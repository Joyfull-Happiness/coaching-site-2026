Hi, I'm Joy

Founder coach. ICF certified. Master NLP coach. Neurology student turned mindset coach. ADHD brain. Podcast host. Faculty at The Multiverse AI School. And the person who's going to sit with you long enough for you to hear what nobody else has said — your neurodivergence is not the reason your company is stalling. The system you're running it on is.

I do the most. I coach neurodivergent founders, I teach at an AI school, I host a podcast, I make music, I'm tapped into the spiritual community — and I'm living my best life doing all of it. With a brain that everyone told me would make this impossible. They were wrong about me. And they're wrong about you too.

I Have All The Interests. I'm Successful In All Of Them. Here's How — With An ADHD Brain.

I'm not going to pretend I picked one lane and stayed in it. I coach founders. I'm faculty at an AI school. I host a podcast. I make music. And somehow — all of it works. With the same brain that can't sit through a meeting without opening 15 browser tabs.

But it didn't always look like this. I used to be the person with 12 ideas before breakfast. Started 8. Finished none. Spent the evening convinced something was fundamentally wrong with me.

The problem was never my brain. It was that every system I tried was designed for a brain I don't have — and I kept blaming myself when it didn't fit.

Here's what I need you to sit with: that voice saying 'maybe I'm just not cut out for this'? That's not reality. That's the gap between how your brain works and how the business world expects you to operate. It's a design mismatch. And design mismatches can be solved.

I studied neurology in college — not because I had some grand plan, but because I wanted to understand why my brain worked the way it did. That background changed everything about how I coach. When I talk about dopamine-driven attention or interest-based motivation, it's not a metaphor. I understand the actual science behind why your brain lights up for a product sprint and shuts down for a board deck. And that's what makes the mindset shifts I facilitate stick — they're not affirmations. They're built on how your nervous system actually processes reward, urgency, and follow-through.

The moment I stopped fighting my ADHD and started building around it — hyperfocus windows, interest-stacking, micro-sprints, radical delegation of low-energy tasks — everything shifted. Not because my brain changed. Because I stopped asking it to be something it's not.

Now I work exclusively with neurodivergent founders. I've coached 100+ of them. They all walk in with the same story: 'I think my ADHD is why I can't scale.' They all leave with a different one.

My Certifications & What They Mean For You

These aren't just letters after my name. Each certification shapes how I coach — and directly impacts the results you get.

ICF

Certified Coach — International Coaching Federation

The ICF is the gold standard in professional coaching worldwide, with over 50,000 members across 150+ countries. My ICF credential means I've completed rigorous coach-specific training, logged 100+ hours of real coaching experience, and passed a comprehensive knowledge assessment. For founders, every session follows evidence-based coaching standards designed for the real pressures of building a company — not generic advice dressed up as coaching. ICF credentialing also requires ongoing ethics training and continuing education, so my practice stays current and accountable.

Master NLP

Master NLP Coach

As a Master NLP coach, I'm trained to spot the patterns you can't see on your own — the shame loop that whispers 'I should be able to do this,' the avoidance around anything with no dopamine reward, the perfectionism that delays a launch by six months. It's why my coaching changes how you relate to your brain... not just what you do with it. NLP is the study of how language and mental patterns shape your behavior, habits, and emotional responses — and it's the foundation of how I help founders rewire the unconscious patterns keeping them stuck.
