The ND Founder Operating Method

Three shifts that turn your neurodivergent brain from your biggest frustration into your actual advantage.

01

Shrink It To A Sprint

If a task can't be started and finished in 15-25 minutes, it's too big for your brain to initiate. That's not weakness — that's how interest-based attention works. We chunk it down.

02

Stack The Dopamine

Your brain runs on interest, not importance. So we pair every low-energy founder task with something your brain actually wants — music, movement, a co-working session. The task gets done. Your brain stays with you.

03

Automate The Follow-Through

ND brains are excellent starters and inconsistent finishers. So we take 'remembering to continue' off your plate — automated check-ins, AI-assisted reminders, calendar architecture that makes follow-through a system, not a memory test.
